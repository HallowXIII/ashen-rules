VITREOUS CELEBRANT -- TIER 4 ELITE CONTROLLER (LEADER)
Size 1 Sentient Construct

A glass priest of the Falcon-Headed Sun, wreathed in splendid, gold-embroidered robes of white silk, wielding a high priest's Crozier in one hand
and a glass incense burner in the other, spreading an intoxicating scent around itself.

HP 80 FP 125
Arm 10 Wrd 25 Eva 6
Initiative 10d6
Movement Speeds: Ground 10

Resistances: Thermal 15

Arc 3 Com 15 Dge 8 Grt 9 Ini 10 Mar 8 Mrk 3 Pot 13 Spe 8 Sta 9

TRAITS

Pure Land Incense: Aura 3, any breathing creature entering or starting its turn in the aura must succeed on a D5 Sta resist or be Dazed (save ends).
Creatures can avoid this effect by holding their breath.

Parma Arcana: Can resist any magical effect with a Pot resist equal to the casting check.

Monkey Grip: Can use 2H weapons in 1 hand.

ACTIONS

Crozier (2 AP): basic melee 10d; 16+1d10 Impact; on hit: D6 Pot resist or lose 32+2d10 FP.

Cast Incense (2 AP): 3 within 12; all creatures in burst are affected as if inside the Pure Land Incense aura.


CASTING (Divine):

Sunfire Blessing (1 AP, 13 FP): 15 to hit 3; 1 ally within 10; target ally's weapon deals an additional 50% (of reliquary) Thermal damage for the rest of the scene.
Tap: Target ally's weapon deals an additional 100% Thermal damage instead.

Aegis of the Faithful (3 AP, 39 FP; Sustain 1 AP, 13 FP): 15 to hit 8; Close Burst 5; All allies within the area gain Resistance 10 to all damage types.
Projectiles and ranged spell attacks that cross the barrier take a -3d penalty to hit. The Celebrant cannot move while sustaining this power.
Tap: The Celebrant may move at half speed while sustaining the aegis.

COMMAND POWERS:
Coordinated Strike (2 AP; 10 FP): 15 to hit 3; 2 allies within 10. You direct two allies to strike in unison. Each ally within
range may immediately make a basic attack as a free action
against a target of your choice. If both allies hit the same target,
that target is Dazed until the end of its next turn.

Hold the Line (2 AP, 20 FP): 15 to hit 7, up to 4 allies within Close Burst 5. You command your allies to form a defensive formation. Up
to four allies within range gain +3 Evasion and +2d on resistance checks until the end of your next turn. While this effect is active,
enemies that move through or out of a square adjacent to an affected ally provoke an opportunity attack.


EQUIPMENT

Glass Crozier; T4 Masterwork Hammer (Flexible); 16+d10 Impact, Disrupting (D7 Pot or 200% FP Damage), Inc 12, 2H, 2 Bulk
Solar Incense; T4 Light Reliquary (Khâru; Sun, Crown); 15+d10 base, Will 4, Favor 1, 1H, 1 Bulk, 3½ Charge, Inc 13

TACTICS

The Celebrant attempts to cover its allies and itself in an _Aegis of the Faithful_ if it can find an advantageous position. While it cannot move, it
uses its command powers to bolster its allies and hold off enemy attacks in melee. It uses its Cast Incense ability to disrupt concentrations of foes.

ECOLOGY

The Vitreous Celebrant acts as a leader of other Glass Servants. It uses its abilities to bolster its allies and hinder its foes.
